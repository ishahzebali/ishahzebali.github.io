CASE 001 Inside the investigation +

The investigation

Reviewed Sysmon and endpoint-related evidence in a simulated enterprise compromise. Decoded Base64 content and reconstructed activity using the Cyber Kill Chain, connecting phishing activity with suspicious PowerShell execution and DNS-tunnelling indicators.

Analysis & documentation

Mapped observed behavior to MITRE ATT&CK, documented detection gaps, and developed practical rule recommendations. The focus was explaining the evidence, not simply listing indicators.

This is a resume-backed project summary, not a full forensic report. Raw logs, exact queries, and detailed technique mappings are not published here. DNS indicators alone do not establish confirmed exfiltration.
